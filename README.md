# 🪄 AIStylist — Your AI-Powered Fashion Companion

**AIStylist** is an intelligent fashion assistant that helps you elevate your style with the power of artificial intelligence.  
Simply take a photo of any clothing item — dress, jacket, shoes, or accessories — and let AIStylist analyze it instantly.  
Our smart image recognition model detects the type, color, and style of your piece, then suggests curated outfit combinations tailored to your taste and trends.

## ✨ Key Features
- 📸 **AI Clothing Detection** — Identify the category and characteristics of any outfit piece in seconds.  
- 👗 **Smart Outfit Recommendations** — Get AI-generated mix-and-match ideas based on your wardrobe or current trends.  
- 🧠 **Personalized Insights** — Save your favorite looks and let AI learn your style preferences over time.  
- 💾 **Digital Wardrobe** — Upload and organize your clothing collection to easily create new combinations.

## 🚀 Built With
- SwiftUI + MVVM architecture  
- Core ML & Vision frameworks for AI-powered image detection  
- Swift Concurrency for smooth, responsive performance
